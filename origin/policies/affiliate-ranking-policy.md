# Affiliate Ranking Policy

Editorial/default user-facing deal rank may use evidence quality, identity confidence, price context, historical context, comparable current offers, condition, shipping/membership clarity, freshness and explicit user preferences.

It may **not** use affiliate commission rate, merchant payout, affiliate-network preference, retailer relationship, sponsor payment, expected revenue per click, or other commercial economics.

Commercial analytics may exist separately for restricted finance/business reporting. Sponsored placements must remain structurally separate from organic ranking, visibly labeled, and disclosed; they never alter the organic deal score.
