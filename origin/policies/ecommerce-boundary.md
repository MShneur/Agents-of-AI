# Ecommerce Boundary

Origin currently acts as **retail intelligence and deal discovery**.

It may model product identity, variants, retailer offers, channel/location context, observations, comparison, deal evaluation, attribution, disclosure, alerts and corrections.

It must not act as merchant of record, payment processor, checkout engine, inventory owner, fulfillment system, returns processor or automated buyer. Commerce frameworks are reference architectures for domain separation, not a reason to import checkout machinery.
