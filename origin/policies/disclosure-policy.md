# Affiliate / Material-Connection Disclosure Policy

When an outbound action has a material affiliate or sponsor relationship, the disclosure must be adjacent to or immediately before the first monetized action, visible without opening a modal/tooltip/footer, understandable to an ordinary user, and repeated when context materially changes.

Default plain-language form:
> Some links may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Commission does not affect our deal score.

Words such as `affiliate`, `commission`, or `disclosure` are not forbidden, but a label that fails to communicate that Origin may be paid is insufficient.

A required but unavailable disclosure blocks publication of the monetized action.
