# Evidence Publication Gate

A public factual observation requires a source URL, observation time, stored content hash/snapshot, acquisition method, parser version, field-level evidence path, schema-valid normalized value, source-policy state, and resolved variant/identity.

Conflicting values suppress certainty. `LLM_assisted` normalization may transform or classify an already evidenced value but may never be the original evidence for price, availability, recall, or identity. Store-local/community observations cannot become retailer-wide claims.
