# B06 Named Practitioner Method Quorum

These practitioners did not participate in or endorse Origin. Their published methods are independent review lenses. Historical fictional Origin personas remain provenance only.

## Method / domain-boundary seat
- **Martin Fowler — Bounded Context / Domain-Driven Design.** Large models should be divided into explicit bounded contexts with clear relationships; the same concept can have different models in different contexts. Source: https://martinfowler.com/bliki/BoundedContext.html
- **Eric Evans — Domain-Driven Design.** Canonical DDD source for entities, value objects, aggregates, ubiquitous language, and strategic bounded contexts; referenced through Fowler's DDD overview and the original Domain-Driven Design work. Source: https://martinfowler.com/bliki/DomainDrivenDesign.html

## Evidence / entity-resolution seat
- **Peter Christen — data matching.** Record linkage requires preprocessing, blocking/indexing, comparison, classification, and measured match quality rather than title equality. Source: https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-642-31164-2
- **William E. Winkler — record linkage.** Multiple identifying fields and explicit match/non-match rules are needed when unique identifiers are missing. Source: https://www.census.gov/library/working-papers/2006/adrm/rrs2006-02.html

## Operator / commerce-architecture seat
- **Sebastian Rindom — modular commerce.** Medusa's public architecture treats core commerce capabilities as composable building blocks/modules with extension points rather than a single overloaded commerce record. Source: https://github.com/medusajs/medusa
- **Patryk Zawadzki — API-first commerce.** Saleor's public architecture is GraphQL-native/API-only and explicitly multichannel, including per-channel control of pricing, currency, stock and products. Source: https://github.com/saleor/saleor

## Adversary / deceptive-commercial-design seat
- **Harry Brignull — deceptive patterns.** A deceptive pattern manipulates or misleads a user into an action they would not otherwise take if they understood the choice. Source: https://www.brignull.com/ and https://deceptive.design/book/contents/get-started/
- **Steve Krug — usability/clarity.** Interfaces should minimize avoidable cognitive friction and make the intended meaning/action obvious rather than requiring users to decode commercial intent. Source: https://sensible.com/dont-make-me-think/

## Affected-user / decision-quality seat
- **Erika Hall — evidence before solutioning.** Ask the real question, identify uncertainty, and gather just enough evidence before turning assumptions into product claims. Source: https://www.muledesign.com/blog/the-9-rules-of-design-research
- **Indi Young — problem-space research.** Understand people's reasoning and decision process before imposing system categories or recommendation logic. Source: https://indiyoung.com/research/

## Synthesis
The quorum converges on explicit domain separation, conservative identity resolution, channel-aware commerce facts, commercial/editorial separation, visible understandable disclosures, and user-decision categories grounded in evidence. No practitioner is represented as having reviewed Origin itself.
