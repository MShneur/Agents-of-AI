# Premature Convergence

## The Failure

Two things collapse a review. Reviewers read each other and drift toward the first confident position, losing the independence that made the panel worth assembling. And once a position is stated with confidence, it stops being revisited even when new evidence arrives.

The output looks like consensus. It is correlation.

## Detection Signal

- Confidence rising while findings fall — the single clearest tell.
- A panel that produced no dissent at all.
- Later reviewers restating earlier reviewers rather than testing them.
- A verdict reached before the hardest question was asked.

## The Fix

Collect positions independently before anyone sees another's. Require every reviewer to name one thing that would change their mind. If the panel converges with no dissent, that is a trigger condition, not a result — assign someone to argue the other side from primary evidence, and reopen.

A review that returns zero findings has measured agreement. Say so in those words.

## Often Mistaken For

Strong consensus, or a well-built plan. The absence of objection is not evidence of soundness.

## When to Check

Every multi-perspective review, and any decision where a panel agreed faster than expected.
