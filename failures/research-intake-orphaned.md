# Research Intake Orphaned

## The Failure

Research collectors, newsletters and technology scouts generate source records, research cards, and promising tool recommendations. The system reports successful discovery but cannot identify a single accepted improvement in its existing agents, workflows, runtime, or project outcomes. Old records repeatedly surface as if they were new. Research activity is mistaken for evolution.

Unlike **Silent Completion**, the source collection may genuinely have succeeded. Unlike **Premature Convergence**, the problem is not overly agreeable review. The failure is the missing transfer from independently sourced mechanism to one owned, evidence-backed capability change.

## Detection Signal

- Many `status:new` source records have no owner disposition or explicit recheck date.
- A donor is "recommended" repeatedly but no comparison to the current seven-layer AoA roster exists.
- The same identifier denotes two different works (`SOURCE_ID_COLLISION`); a merge risks losing provenance.
- Researchers record tests performed on demos but never show owner/target integration or outcome.
- A source has been known for weeks while the finding remains `UNCONSUMED`.

## The Fix

1. Use the EXISTING research registry and reject memory; do not launch another collector or create another master index.
2. For each material candidate pin source/date/version, license/rights or UNKNOWN, mechanism signature, source identity, observed feature and limitations.
3. Check whether the exact capability already exists in AoA, R&Duck, CTRL-AI or the named project. Merge same method into the existing owner; add a new seven-layer entry only for materially different behavior.
4. Assign one owner and next executable comparison or implementation. If owner rights or source approval are missing, mark `HOLD` explicitly.
5. Distinguish `DISCOVERED`, `SOURCE_VERIFIED`, `OWNER_STAGED`, `INTEGRATED`, `HOST_ACCEPTED`, `RELEASED`. A research score or synthetic unit PASS earns no host-integration credit.
6. Advance a coherent authorized build before broad discretionary testing. Use one consolidated outcome acceptance and preserve genuine access/security/human gates.
7. Record real adoption lag or status `UNKNOWN`; avoid reporting an unearned improvement count.

## When to Use

A cross-project R&D Radar, Origin transfer, scout discovery batch, or user request to upgrade **all** relevant agents and protocols from existing research.

## When NOT to Use

Quick factual research, one-off literature questions, pending unapproved source access, or a project where the improvement has already been accepted and measured.

## Acceptance

Given multiple stored source leads, the operator reports one disposition and one correct existing owner per unique mechanism. Duplicated source IDs are flagged without inventing attribution. At least one genuinely approved opportunity is either transferred into a specific real owner change or accurately marked as blocked; no new scheduler, agent manager or competing research database appears.
